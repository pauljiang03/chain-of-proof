# Sol 6.1 pilot result: with and without Lean feedback

The pilot completed using two fresh subagents requested as `gpt-6.1-sol`, one
without semantic checker feedback (A) and one with the existing online Lean
feedback workflow (C). Both received the same extracted NIST evidence, public
case and questions, and a 900-second controller cap. A ran first, then C, then
offline replay of A's exact proposals (B). This is one development pair, not a
statistically established performance result.

## Literal answer agreement

Both final model answer blocks passed the public submission contract and were
scored unchanged with the same deterministic evaluator. The frozen draft key
has 46 provisionally scorable questions and 14 unresolved questions. Both answer
blocks remain unverified; certificates do not prove that all answer values follow.

| Measure | Without Lean feedback (A) | With Lean feedback (C) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fully correct, among 46 scorable | 11 | 12 |
| Partially correct | 11 | 7 |
| Unanswered | 23 | 27 |
| Incorrect | 1 | 0 |
| Unresolved reference questions | 14 | 14 |
| Matched atomic answers, among 1,627 scorable | 814 | 238 |
| Missing atomic answers | 813 | 1,389 |
| Extra or unexpected atoms | 4,959 | 26 |
| Unequal-value atoms | 134 | 25 |
| Controller elapsed seconds | 851.97 | 593.40 |

C produced one additional fully correct question, fewer contract mismatches,
and less answer coverage. Neither arm clearly dominates completeness and
agreement. C's additional fully correct question was Q18; A's additional partial
coverage was Q11, Q12, Q15 and Q23.

Atomic totals heavily weight large lists. A's Q11/Q12/Q15 account for 5,003 of its
5,093 mismatched atoms. Extra assertions under this contract are not independently
established factual errors: aggregates cannot distinguish redundant detail,
schema deviations, scope differences and substantive errors. These metrics are
not forensic precision or false-positive rates.

## Claim checks

- A submitted 171 exact candidates. B certified 169, and all 169 passed independent
  verification. Replay preserved duplicates, order and whole conjunctions, with
  zero model calls and no repairs.
- Both B refusals were capacity limits. The proposed counts, 518 `programRun`
  records and 747 `webVisit` records, matched the checker's observed counts; its
  census-proof limit is 500 records. These refusals did not demonstrate detected
  factual errors.
- C made 32 check calls: 30 verified responses representing 29 unique retained
  certificates and one duplicate, plus the same two capacity refusals. All 29
  retained certificates passed independent verification.
- B has no question-accuracy score. The shared claim-to-answer projector remains
  unfinished, as does the broader A/B/C/D protocol.

The live bridge logs contain 295 calls for A and 108 for C, with zero transport
or bridge errors. Both investigators finished voluntarily before their cap.
